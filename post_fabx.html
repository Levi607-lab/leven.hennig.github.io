Fabx: Bridging the Gap

My journey at Fabx 2025.

Fabx 2025 was a unique opportunity to connect with makers, educators, and innovators from around the world. The event focused on bridging the gap between technology and society, and I was able to contribute by sharing my own experiences and learning from others.

During the conference, I participated in workshops, panel discussions, and hands-on sessions that explored the latest trends in digital fabrication, open source hardware, and collaborative learning. The atmosphere was inspiring and encouraged everyone to think beyond traditional boundaries.

One of the highlights was working on a collaborative project that brought together people from different backgrounds to solve real-world challenges using maker tools and creative thinking. This experience reinforced my belief in the power of community and open knowledge sharing.

Fabx 2025 not only expanded my technical skills but also deepened my understanding of how making can drive positive change. I left the event with new friends, fresh ideas, and a renewed passion for building bridges between people and technology.
